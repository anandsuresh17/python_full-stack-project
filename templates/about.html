WHO WE ARE

SCOPE INDIA, Your Career Partner

SCOPE INDIA is a professional education centre in Kerala and Tamil Nadu from Suffix E Solutions, an IT company, dedicated to helping students build successful careers in the IT industry through practical learning and industry-focused training.

With 19 years of experience in professional education, SCOPE INDIA has become a preferred choice for students who want to gain real-world technical skills and enhance their career opportunities.

SCOPE INDIA focuses on a job-oriented curriculum that helps students adapt to new technologies and understand how real IT companies work.

The training approach goes beyond classroom theory. Students get opportunities to work on live projects, internships and practical assignments that improve their technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities and hands-on experience.

CAREER SUPPORT

Support Throughout Your Career Journey

One of the biggest advantages of learning at SCOPE INDIA is the placement support provided to students who successfully complete their training programs.

From resume preparation and interview guidance to placement assistance, students receive support throughout their career journey.

SCOPE INDIA also believes in building long-term relationships with students by providing continued career guidance, professional assistance and skill support whenever needed.

REAL-WORLD EXPERIENCE

On-the-Job Training

SCOPE INDIA provides On-the-Job Training for students who have completed their course. During this period, students get opportunities to work on real-time and commercial projects from Suffix E Solutions, an IT company.

This experience helps students understand how real projects are planned, developed, tested and delivered. Students get exposure to the complete project lifecycle and gain a better understanding of professional project environments.

Students also gain valuable project experience during their internship period, helping them improve problem-solving abilities, teamwork and practical implementation skills.

OUR STORY

From Experience to Education

2007

Founded

Suffix E Solutions was founded with a focus on networking and software services. The company developed experience across different stages of the software development lifecycle, including documentation, framework design, coding, testing and maintenance.

2015

SCOPE INDIA Launched

SCOPE INDIA was launched by Suffix E Solutions to address the growing demand for industry-relevant IT training under experienced working professionals.

2019

Kochi Expansion

SCOPE INDIA expanded to Kochi to provide more aspiring professionals with access to practical technology education.

2021

Nagercoil Branch

The Nagercoil branch was launched, expanding the reach of SCOPE INDIA to more students in Tamil Nadu.

2023

Technopark Branch

SCOPE INDIA launched its Technopark branch, bringing its training programs closer to the heart of Kerala's IT hub.
